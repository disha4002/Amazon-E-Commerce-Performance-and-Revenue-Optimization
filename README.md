# Amazon-E-Commerce-Performance-and-Revenue-Optimization
## Project Background
This dataset contains 100,000 synthetic Amazon-style e-commerce sales transactions, designed to closely resemble real-world online retail behavior. This dataset is sued for E-Commerce Performance and Revenue Optimization answering business problem- Why is Amazon's revenue declining? 
<br>
Here the main question is that: How can Amazon identify the key drivers of revenue performance and optimize customers, products, pricing, sellers and operations to improve overall business performance?
<br>
We create four areas to analyse this:
<br>
① Revenue
<br>
Where is revenue coming from and where is it weakening?
<br>
② Customer
<br>
Which customers/regions are driving business value?
<br>
③ Product & Pricing
<br>
Which products/categories/brands perform best, and is discounting effective?
<br>
④ Operations
<br>
Where are cancellations, returns and shipping costs hurting performance?

## Overview
The chart below shows the monthly revenue trend for the year. A key observation is the sharp decline in revenue from January to February. Revenue decreased from approximately ₹78.84 lakh in January to ₹69.32 lakh in February, representing a decline of ₹9.52 lakh (12.1%).
<br>
Revenue recovered in March and remained relatively stable throughout the rest of the year, with only moderate fluctuations.
<br>
The next step is to investigate the root cause of the February decline by analyzing factors such as order volume, product/category performance, quantity sold, and order status.
<br>
Now the problem is to find the root cause behind that decline in that sspecific month.
